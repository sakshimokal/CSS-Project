ABOUT US

The Story of Preps & Fiesta Productions

Preps & Fiesta began with one simple belief every celebration deserves to be unforgettable. What started as a small team with big dreams quickly grew into a full-fledged event production company built on creativity, passion, and precision.

From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, we’ve turned countless visions into magical realities. Our team brings together planners, designers, and dreamers who live for the joy of creating moments that matter.

At Preps & Fiesta, every event is more than just a party it’s a story waiting to be told, and we make sure yours shines brighter than ever.
